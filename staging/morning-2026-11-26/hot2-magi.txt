Both carry a rank range of 1 to 3. That is a statistical tie for first place. The price gap is real at 1080p and shrinks fast at lower resolutions.</p>

<h2>The tie, in numbers</h2>
<p>Artificial Analysis ranks image-to-video models by Elo from blind pairwise votes. Voters compare videos made from the same input image and pick the result they prefer. A five-point Elo gap converts to a 50.7 percent expected win rate for the leader under the standard Elo formula. A coin flip sits at 50 percent. The confidence ranges overlap across 23 points, from 1149 to 1172. MAGI-2 also leads the silent image-to-video board at 1153 Elo. Its preview scored 967 Elo, so the full release gained 196 points over the preview on the audio board.</p>
<div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bh-img-assets/byteherald-images/main/img/m1126-hot2-magi-chart.png" alt="Chart: image-to-video Elo scores with confidence ranges showing MAGI-2 and Seedance 2.5 tied at the top" /></div>
<p>A second board disagrees. <a href="https://higgsfield.ai/magi-2-intro">Higgsfield published a Bradley-Terry evaluation on November 24, 2026</a> with Seedance 2.5 first at 1,219 points and MAGI-2 second at 1,146 points among six models. MAGI-2 won 39 percent of head-to-head comparisons against Seedance 2.5 in that evaluation, while beating Kling 4.0, MiniMax H3, Wan 3.0, and Seedance 2.0. Different voters, prompts, and scoring produce different winners. Buyers who animate still images should treat quality as tied until their own prompts say otherwise. Neither board tests a buyer's exact shots, faces, or dialogue.</p>
<h2>The price gap depends on resolution</h2>
<p>Artificial Analysis normalizes API pricing to one minute at benchmark settings, 1080p where offered. MAGI-2 lists at 4.80 dollars per minute. Seedance 2.5 lists at 34.12 dollars per minute. The ratio is 7.1 to 1. MAGI-2 charges a flat 0.08 dollars per second at 1080p, so a 30-second clip costs 2.40 dollars. <a href="https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/modelark/model-pricing">Seedance bills by video tokens on BytePlus ModelArk</a>. Its published 16:9 examples without video input work out to about 0.103 dollars per second at 480p, 0.231 dollars at 720p, and 0.569 dollars at 1080p. The table below multiplies those list rates by clip length for one successful generation.</p>

<p>Three limits keep the table honest. First, Seedance token billing counts input video seconds as well as output seconds, so reference-video jobs cost more than the no-input baseline. Second, discarded takes cost full price on both routes. Third, list prices move. <a href="https://higgsfield.ai/blog/best-ways-to-access-magi-2">Higgsfield sells MAGI-2 through its web app at 75 credits for 30 seconds</a>, about 3.69 dollars on its Plus plan, which runs about 1.5 times the API rate. Higgsfield also reported on November 25, 2026 that Sand.ai top-ups were not supported yet. A buyer without existing Sand.ai credits may need a partner API first. <a href="https://www.byteherald.com/2026/10/gpt-6-vs-claude-vs-gemini-october-2026.html">Our October price tracker used the same rule for language models</a>: compare normalized cost at the settings the buyer will actually run, never a headline ratio alone.</p>
<h2>Which workflow each model fits</h2>
<p>MAGI-2 fits image-first work at 1080p. Sand.ai built a 480 billion parameter model with about 28 billion parameters active per token. Clips run from 4 to 30 seconds at 1080p and 25 frames per second, with dialogue, sound effects, ambient sound, and music generated with the video. One request can mix up to 10 images, 5 videos, and 5 audio clips as references. The flat rate removes resolution math. It also removes the cheap draft tier. Every test take renders at full 1080p price.</p>
<p>Seedance 2.5 fits tiered work. A team can draft at 480p for about 1.3 times the MAGI-2 rate, review motion, then pay 1080p prices only for finals. That draft path matters when most takes fail. At 100 draft clips of 10 seconds, MAGI-2 costs 80 dollars at list. Seedance drafts at 480p cost about 103 dollars, then finals at 1080p cost about 5.69 dollars each. MAGI-2 wins when the first take ships. Seedance narrows the gap when the workflow burns takes. Cheaper boards exist below the tie. Boreal-H3 scores 1145 Elo at 2.42 dollars per minute on Artificial Analysis, and HeyGen Video 1 scores 1123 at 2.40 dollars per minute. Both trail the leaders on quality. Both beat MAGI-2 on price alone.</p>
