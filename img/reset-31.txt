It does not by itself prove that the market will sustain any particular valuation after trading begins, and it does not convert offering proceeds into operating performance. The base offering includes shares sold by selling stockholders, so the $81,340,000,000 total offering price is not cash received by Anthropic. The company-sold portion is 956,351,354 shares before discounts, commissions and expenses.</p><p>Analysis: the November filings answer the disclosure question that October left open. They do not answer the durability question.
