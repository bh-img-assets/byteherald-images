<p>ByteHerald finding: the October 2026 claim that three labs share one mid tier price still holds at list price, but it stops holding once cache reads, prompt length thresholds, scheduled increases and token counting are applied. Rebuilding the October tracker from the three provider pricing pages checked November 27, 2026 confirms a $2.00 input and $10.00 output cluster for GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5.5, and Gemini 4 Argon, yet computations show Argon leaves that cluster on December 1, 2026, Claude Haiku 5.5 costs 5 times more per blended million tokens once a prompt passes 100,000 tokens, and Claude prices from version 4.7 onward buy about 30 percent more tokens for the same text. List parity is real. Bill parity is not.</p>
<h2>How ByteHerald built this ledger</h2>
<p>ByteHerald recorded Standard tier paid prices per million tokens from the three provider pricing pages, each checked November 27, 2026. Blended cost means 750,000 input tokens plus 250,000 output tokens at list rates, excluding cache writes, batch discounts, tools, and negotiated rates unless a row states otherwise. Scheduled rows use only dates printed on provider pages. The tokenizer adjustment multiplies a Claude blended price by 1.30 to express cost per fixed amount of text on the older tokenizer basis, using Anthropic guidance that Claude 4.7 and later models produce approximately 30 percent more tokens for the same text. That adjustment is analysis, not a provider price. This ledger compares prices, not capability.</p>

<h2>Three findings</h2>
<p>First, the $2.00 and $10.00 cluster has an expiry date. Google prints Argon at $2.00 and $10.00 through November 30, 2026 and $4.00 and $20.00 from December 1, 2026, moving its blended cost from $4.00 to $8.00 beside Claude Opus 5.5 rather than beside GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Sonnet 5.5. The October tracker treated the Argon increase as undated. The provider page now dates it, so budgets built on October parity need the December rate. Gemini 3.8 Flash doubles later, from $1.50 through December 31, 2026 to $3.00 from January 1, 2027, staying below the $4.00 cluster after the increase. Analysis: the labs matched headline pairs in October, but Google matched with a timer attached.</p>
<p>Second, cache reads and routing widen small list differences. GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 tie at $20.00 blended, yet cached input costs $1.00 per million on Astra against $0.25 on Fable 5.1, a 4 times gap on repeated context. The mid tier ties at $0.10 cache reads for GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5.5, and Argon through November 30, 2026. Routing then moves two labs together: OpenAI charges a 10 percent regional uplift for models released on or after March 5, 2026, and Anthropic applies a 1.1 times multiplier for United States only inference on Claude 4.6 and later models, moving a $4.00 blended cost to $4.40. Anthropic lists Opus 5 at $5.00 and $25.00 beside Opus 5.5 at $4.00 and $20.00, a 20 percent cut, and Haiku 4.5 at $1.00 and $5.00 beside Haiku 5.5 short prompt pricing, a 90 percent cut that narrows past 100,000 tokens.</p>
<p>Third, token counting changes what a Claude price buys. Anthropic states the tokenizer in Claude 4.7 and later models produces approximately 30 percent more tokens for the same text. On a fixed text basis, the $4.00 blended cost for Claude Sonnet 5.5 becomes about $5.20 per older tokenizer equivalent million tokens, and $8.00 for Claude Opus 5.5 becomes about $10.40. ByteHerald labels this a text volume adjustment only. It does not mean a task costs 30 percent more, because newer models may need fewer turns or output tokens to finish the same work. The October spread needs its condition too: $20.00 divided by $0.20 equals 100 times only at short prompt list rates. With Haiku 5.5 over 100,000 tokens at $1.00 blended, the spread against the flagship tier falls to 20 times on that row.</p>
<h2>Limits: what this ledger does not establish</h2>
<p>This ledger does not establish which model finishes a task cheapest. It excludes thinking token volume differences beyond the rates providers fold into output pricing, tool and grounding charges, batch and priority tiers, negotiated discounts, and tokens spent to reach an acceptable answer. Google output prices include thinking tokens. Two increases here take effect within weeks of the check date. Readers should confirm the live provider page before budgeting and measure cost per completed task. ByteHerald will keep the October baseline frozen and record future moves as dated rows.</p>
