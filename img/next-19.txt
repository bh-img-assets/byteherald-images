Company claims inside releases, such as customer counts, are company figures and are not independently verified here. The ledger does not establish that these six rounds represent October funding as a whole, and announced amounts can differ from closed amounts. What it establishes is narrower and citable: how much was announced, by whom, and how much of the deal each announcement chose to disclose.
