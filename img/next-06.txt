<h2>Analysis</h2>
<p>Analysis: Team and Enterprise are not two prices for the same risk posture. Team begins with the feature on and outside guests allowed, so a small organization can have Claude in a client call before an owner has reviewed guest handling. Enterprise begins with the feature off and outside guests blocked until an owner allows them, so the first decision is explicit. That difference matters because Claude does more than summarize after the fact. It takes notes, answers questions, and builds artifacts such as docs, whiteboards, and slides live during the call, according to Anthropic's Help Center.</p>
<p>The guest rules are more nuanced than a simple allow or block switch. When outside guests are allowed, Anthropic says Claude listens and takes notes, posts its notice in call chat, and does not reply there; guest speech is included in notes and transcript, while guests do not get the meeting page or recap email. When outside guests are not allowed, Claude stays out of calls with outside invitees and leaves if an outsider joins partway through, including another company's notetaker bot. Those rules reduce surprise, but they do not remove the need for meeting consent norms inside each organization.</p>
<p>Pricing is temporarily simple and later uncertain. Anthropic says notes, transcripts, and answers that do not use connected tools are included on Team and Enterprise plans. Docs and whiteboards made in a meeting are free through February 10, 2027. Connected-tool use counts toward usage and can lead to charges. The beta therefore subsidizes artifact creation for a bounded window while keeping tool-mediated work inside normal usage accounting. Organizations should not treat the February 10, 2027 date as a permanent price.</p>
<h2>What the evidence does not establish</h2>
<p>The evidence does not establish transcription accuracy, artifact quality, retention period, deletion workflow, legal compliance for regulated meetings, or support for platforms beyond Google Meet. It also does not establish post-beta pricing. The Help Center states that Claude saves a transcript, summary, and screen descriptions, not audio. That is a useful privacy boundary, but it is not a complete data-governance record.</p>
<p>What to watch next is whether Anthropic publishes retention and deletion terms in the same Help Center record, names additional meeting platforms with dates, and states post-beta artifact pricing before February 10, 2027. For administrators, the immediate record to check is the Organization settings page for Claude Meetings, because the default state differs by plan and the guest setting can change what Claude hears.</p>
