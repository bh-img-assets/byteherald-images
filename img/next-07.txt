The story is a divergence between visits and referrals, not a uniform slide.</p>

<h2>What the ledger shows</h2>
<p>April 2026 is the shared low point. Similarweb gave ChatGPT 53.7% of visits in April 2026 and 56.4% in September 2026, a gain of 2.7 points by ByteHerald calculation. The post-April recovery appears in both panels, even though the panels count different actions.</p>
<p>Gemini moved in opposite directions after April 2026. Its visit share fell from 26.7% in April 2026 to 26.6% in July 2026, 25.6% in August 2026, and 25.0% in September 2026 (Similarweb, 12 October 2026). Its referral share rose from 9.00% in April 2026 (Statcounter, 7 May 2026) to 10.91% in September 2026 (Statcounter Global Stats, September 2026). Claude diverged the other way: visits rose from 8.0% in April 2026 to 9.6% in September 2026, while referrals fell from 2.66% in April 2026 to 0.95% in September 2026. The ledger keeps the two windows separate.</p>
<h2>Methodology</h2>
<p>Similarweb published five comparison points for seven sites on 12 October 2026: October 2025, April 2026, July 2026, August 2026, and September 2026. Statcounter published its monthly referral table through April 2026 on 7 May 2026. Statcounter Global Stats data for September 2026 supplied the September referral shares. ByteHerald computed point changes by subtraction and ratios by division, rounded to two decimal places. No secondary table or chart image was used as evidence.</p>
<h2>Analysis</h2>
<p>Analysis: ChatGPT led on both rulers in September 2026 and had regained ground since April 2026 on both. Gemini was second on both rulers, but its direction since April 2026 depended on the ruler. Claude was third on visits and fifth on referrals in September 2026, so a ranking from one panel cannot be carried to the other without restating what was counted. The 56.4% figure is a share of website visits among seven listed sites, not a share of users, conversations, revenue, or model quality.</p>
<h2>What the evidence does not establish</h2>
<p>Neither primary input assigns a cause for any movement. Similarweb counts website visits (12 October 2026), so app use, API use, and AI inside other products are outside that measure. Statcounter counts referrals to websites, and its 7 May 2026 release states that its data is based on over 3 billion page views per month to over 1 million websites, so a chatbot that answers without sending readers to websites can score low on referrals while still being used. The panels cover different site sets because Statcounter excludes Grok. Referral figures can be revised after first publication, so second-decimal differences are not exact. The ledger does not prove user counts, retention, revenue, or which assistant is better.</p>
