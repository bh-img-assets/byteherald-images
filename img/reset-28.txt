<h2>What the flow means</h2>
<p>Our count for the window since October 16, 2026 is two researcher departures and one compute-lead role ending, against one senior return and three hires from Anthropic. The departures concentrate in research and compute leadership. Yin is a theoretical physicist who joined in late May 2026 and announced his departure on November 3, 2026. Wang led ChatGPT Voice development and demonstrated it at the GPT-5 launch, according to DeepTech reporting carried on November 25 and 26, 2026. Katti led infrastructure and compute for roughly a year, with his last day in the role on November 15, 2026 and Uday Ruddarraju taking over the compute organization, CNBC reported on October 14, 2026.</p>
<p>The arrivals concentrate in product and growth. Cat Wu led product for Claude Code and announced on November 5, 2026 that she would lead product management for Core Products at OpenAI. Austin Lau was Anthropic's first growth marketer and announced the same day that he had joined OpenAI. Thariq Shihipar worked on Claude Code for more than a year and announced on November 25, 2026 that he had joined OpenAI, without naming a role. Aleksander Madry's return adds a senior safety and security figure back to OpenAI.</p>
<p>Analysis: the ledger does not support a simple brain-drain story after November. It supports a rotation story. OpenAI is losing named research and compute figures while hiring the people who shipped a rival's developer product. That pattern can strengthen product execution while weakening research depth, or it can be ordinary churn at a large lab. The ledger cannot decide motive, because most rows record moves, not reasons.</p>
<p>What the ledger does not establish is net headcount, unannounced departures, or Wang's next employer. It also does not count rumored moves. Anthony Morris was reported by an anonymous account on November 17, 2026 to have joined OpenAI, but neither Morris nor OpenAI confirmed it in the record we reviewed, so he is excluded. The citable finding is the split itself: research and compute out, product and growth in, with evidence labels separating confirmed moves from reported ones.</p>
