CISA set October 29, 2026 as the due date for federal civilian agencies and required forensic triage under BOD 26-04. Analysis: a KEV listing does not prove that any particular site was compromised. It does prove that exploitation evidence was strong enough for CISA to impose a federal deadline.</p><h2>The Check Point chain narrows the claim</h2><p>Check Point's November 12, 2026 advisory reported a more specific chain: PHP2Shell gave initial access through the Mobile Access Portal, then CVE-2026-123039, a local privilege escalation in the Gaia OS Dynamic CLI Service rated CVSS 7.8, was used to obtain root privileges and establish persistence. Check Point said exploitation activity dated back to October 30, 2026 and was observed as active until November 4, 2026. It recommended installing the November Jumbo Hotfix on Management Servers and Security Gateways.</p><p>Analysis: the correct update is narrower than a web-wide compromise claim and stronger than the disclosure-day warning. PHP2Shell was patched on October 21, 2026, listed by CISA as known exploited on October 26, 2026, and observed by Check Point in a root-level gateway chain beginning October 30, 2026. The dividing line is not application code quality. It is whether the PHP runtime and any embedded gateway PHP surface were patched before exploitation reached them.</p><p>How to cite this timeline: cite October 21, 2026 for patch availability, October 26, 2026 for CISA known-exploited status, and November 12, 2026 for Check Point's observed gateway chain.
