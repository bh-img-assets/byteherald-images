<p>ByteHerald's eligibility matrix finds that claiming a business in ChatGPT is a two-gate process, and the second gate is the one that decides everything. A claim alone unlocks almost nothing: OpenAI requires Persona identity verification before a business can publish changes, view insights, connect a product feed, or create an agent, according to OpenAI's Help page current as of November 28, 2026. Businesses that treat the claim as the finish line will sit in a dashboard they cannot use.</p>

<h2>What the matrix shows</h2>
<p>Eligibility split by business type on November 19, 2026, when OpenAI updated its announcement to state that ecommerce and online service businesses outside the US are eligible to claim and publish agents. Local stores and restaurants remain narrower: they can currently claim only an existing US listing. A claim can be proven four ways, by phone, business email, website domain, or Shopify.</p>
<p>After the claim, Persona verification gates four actions: publishing changes, viewing insights, connecting a product feed, and creating an eligible business agent. Insights, once unlocked, show aggregate mentions and website clicks for the past 28 days. Two non-guarantees matter for planning. Connecting a product feed does not publish an agent, and it does not guarantee recommendations. An agent itself requires four conditions together: an eligible ecommerce or online service business, a website, claim plus Persona approval, and compliance with the Business Agents Policy.</p>
<p>The reach figure framing the launch, 1.2 billion people who use ChatGPT each week, comes from OpenAI's November 10, 2026 announcement. It describes the platform audience, not the audience any single business will see. The 28-day aggregate insights window is the first place a business can measure its own share of that attention.</p>
<h2>Methodology and limits</h2>
<p>Methodology: we coded each published rule from the announcement, the November 19, 2026 update, and the Help page into decision points and checked the matrix for contradictions between the announcement and the Help rules. Limits: OpenAI does not publish approval rates for Persona verification, recommendation criteria, or how mentions are counted in insights. Rules are changing during rollout, as the November 19 eligibility expansion shows, so the matrix is a snapshot dated November 28, 2026. The evidence does not establish that claiming improves visibility for any specific business.</p>
<h2>Analysis</h2>
<p>Analysis: the structure favors businesses that complete verification early, because insights data accumulates only after the Persona gate opens. The feed non-guarantee is the honest part of the design: OpenAI is selling presence and measurement first, with agent publishing as a separate compliance step. Local businesses outside the US face the longest wait, since their category is limited to existing US listings while online sellers worldwide are already eligible.</p>
