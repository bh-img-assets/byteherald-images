<p>This log records corrections to published ByteHerald articles. Each entry states the article, what changed, and when it changed. Minor spelling and formatting fixes may be made without an entry. Material corrections are always logged here.</p>
<h2>How to report an error</h2>
<p>If you find an error in a ByteHerald article, tell us through the Contact page with the article, the statement in dispute, and a reliable source that supports the concern. We check every report against the cited source, correct the article visibly where it is wrong, and add an entry below. Our verification process is described on our <a href="https://www.byteherald.com/p/methodology.html">Methodology</a> page.</p>
